How to use

  1. Bookmark this page. Open it any time on your phone or laptop — no install, no login, no code.
  2. Green badge = price inside the safe $0.99–$1.01 band. Red = depeg down. Amber = depeg up.
  3. Volume spike or cross-source banners appear at the top when stress is detected.
  4. Leave it open. It refreshes itself every 30 seconds. If a peg breaks, you see it immediately.
  5. For alerts on your phone, ask your technical team to enable Telegram or email in the Python monitor.
