How to use

  1. Bookmark this page. Open it any time on your phone or laptop — no install, no login, no code required.
  2. Green badge = price inside the safe $0.99–$1.01 band. Red = depeg down. Amber = depeg up. Gray = no data.
  3. Volume spike or depeg banners appear at the top when stress is detected. Leave the page open; it refreshes itself every 30 seconds.
  4. The thin bar under each price shows distance from the $1.00 peg. The center mark is exactly $1.00. Sparklines show recent price path.
  5. For phone alerts, ask your technical team to enable Telegram or email in the Python monitor (depeg_detector.py).
