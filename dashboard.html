Stablecoin Depeg Intelligence

Real-time peg monitoring for funds, prop desks, and fintech teams

How to use

Open this file in any browser (including iPhone Safari). It refreshes every 30 seconds from CoinGecko. Green badges mean the coin is within $0.99–$1.01. Red means a depeg down; amber means a depeg up. Volume spikes (current volume more than 3 imes the recent average) appear as a highlighted banner at the top. Bookmark it. No install, no login, no code required. Just open and watch.
